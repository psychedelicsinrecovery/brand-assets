# 📘 PIR® Brand Guidelines

*Draft. Plain-language guidance from PIR's PR committee. Not legal advice.*

## The marks

- **PIR®:** the registered mark.
- **Psychedelics In Recovery™:** the name, used as a trademark.
- **The eye-in-diamond icon**, alone or with lettering.

Use the ® or ™ symbol on the **first prominent use** in a piece (for example a title or logo).
Running text after that can drop it.

## ✅ Please do

- Use the official files from this repo, unmodified.
- Leave clear space around the logo, at least the height of the "I" in **PIR**.
- Use the navy version on light backgrounds and the white version on dark backgrounds.
- Refer to us as **PIR®** or **Psychedelics In Recovery™**.

## 🚫 Please don't

- Stretch, recolor, rotate, add effects, or redraw the marks.
- Combine our marks with another organization's logo in a way that implies endorsement or
  affiliation, or any connection to a commercial product.
- Use the marks to suggest PIR endorses a treatment, substance, provider, retreat or event.
  Under our traditions, PIR doesn't endorse or affiliate with outside enterprises.
- Sell merchandise bearing our marks without written permission from PIR.

## Who may use the marks

- **PIR groups, committees and service members:** yes, for PIR service work.
- **Press and educators:** yes, to refer to PIR accurately.
- **Anything else** (merch, partnerships, commercial use): ask **pr@psychedelicsinrecovery.org**
  first.
